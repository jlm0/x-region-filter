X Region FilterHelp & privacy

Choose what reaches your feed.

Hide posts by an account's region or age, including reposts and quoted authors.

Set your filters

Open the extension to add a country or region. The default list contains India and Africa. “Include countries within each region” also matches Africa's countries and subregions. The separate age switch hides accounts created less than three calendar months ago.

Changes save automatically. The switch at the top pauses filtering and restores posts while keeping your choices.

Where it works

The extension filters Home, including For you and Following, and List timelines. A match for the author, original author, visible reposter, or any visible quoted author hides the whole item. Ordinary mentions do not count. Profiles, individual posts, search, notifications, and messages remain readable.

In your feed

Post hidden by region filter

A small placeholder explains which rule hid the post. X keeps its feed rows and scrolling controls. Hidden videos pause; restoring a post does not restart playback.

Check whether it's working

Open the popup while viewing Home or a List. It shows the number of posts hidden in that feed and the authors waiting for a check. These counts describe the rows X currently has on the page, so they can decrease as you scroll.

Expand “Activity & details” for the latest account lookup, known locations and ages, and X's remaining About allowance. “Refresh status” reads local extension status; it does not request an account lookup.

Why some posts stay visible

Unfamiliar accounts wait for a lookup. Checks run one at a time, with at least 30 seconds between them and at most 20 starts per rolling 15 minutes. X shares its About allowance with your manual About-page visits. The extension keeps a reserve and pauses when capacity is low or X returns an error. Cached matches still hide during a pause.

Rapid scrolling can bring in authors faster than the extension can check them. Missing labels or creation dates leave posts visible. Only author headers X renders can be checked, including those in quote chains.

If the popup says it is waiting for X, reload your signed-in Home or List tab. If it reports a cooldown, wait until the displayed time. Clearing the cache does not reset a cooldown. X can change its page or endpoint, which may require an extension update.

How regions and ages match

Regions use X's “Account based in” label. This is an account-level label, not a person's nationality or precise location. Country groups follow UN M49. North America includes Northern America, Central America, and the Caribbean. Custom labels, including Middle East, match exactly. Selecting India does not match an account labeled only Asia or South Asia.

Age comes from the same About response as the region. A matching account becomes eligible at its three-month anniversary in UTC, with month-end dates clamped to the last valid day. Its posts restore then unless another rule still matches. Invalid, missing, or future dates do not trigger the age rule. Older cached results without a date wait for their normal refresh.

Your data stays in your browser

The extension stores your filters, cached account labels and creation dates, and request-limit state locally. Known account metadata expires after six hours; results with neither field expire after five minutes. The cache holds at most 2,000 accounts.

To use your existing X session, the extension observes X's Authorization header and keeps it in session-only extension storage. Each account lookup reads the current CSRF cookie and sends both values only to X. The CSRF value is not saved. The extension has no analytics, outside lookup service, or cloud sync, and never asks for your password.

It does not rewrite X's responses, replace its network functions, or mute or block accounts on X. “Clear account cache” removes the remembered account data, while your settings and request limits remain. Uninstalling removes extension storage; reload X afterward to clear the page's injected styles.

Install or update

  1. Keep the project or extracted extension folder on your computer.
  2. Open chrome://extensions or brave://extensions and enable Developer mode.
  3. Choose “Load unpacked” and select the extension folder containing manifest.json.
  4. Open or reload your signed-in X feed. Pin the extension for quick access.

For updates, replace the files at the installed path, reload the extension on your browser's extensions page, then reload X. Use one enabled copy at a time. Your settings stay with the installed extension ID; loading from a different path can create a separate copy.
