Feed filters, explained.

Regions and account age

Add countries or regions to match X’s “Account based in” label. “South Asia” and “Southern Asia” are equivalent. Region expansion includes countries and subregions. The age switch hides accounts under three calendar months old.

Posts, reposts and quotes

Home and List feeds collapse matching posts into a placeholder. A match for any visible author, reposter or quoted author hides the whole item. The toolbar badge counts currently hidden posts in that tab.

The top switch pauses filtering. Profiles, search and individual posts stay readable.

Accounts waiting for a check

Unknown accounts stay visible. Checks run at least 30 seconds apart, up to 20 per 15 minutes. X’s limits can pause checks; cached matches still hide.

Opening an account’s About page also saves its displayed region, without a second request. English About labels are supported. Reload X after an extension update.

Privacy

Settings, account metadata and request limits stay in your browser. Session credentials go only to X. No analytics, cloud sync or outside lookup service. Clearing the cache keeps your filters and cooldown.

Install or update

In chrome://extensions or brave://extensions, enable Developer mode and load the project’s extension folder. To update an existing installation, replace its files, reload the extension, then reload X.
